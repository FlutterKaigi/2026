# Sign in with Google assets

`g-logo.png` is Google's official G logo used by the custom-width button:

https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png

The other PNG files are the unmodified 4x, square, text-enabled buttons from
Google's pre-approved Sign in with Google asset bundle and are retained as
brand reference assets:

https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip

Downloaded on 2026-07-30. Keep the artwork unmodified.
